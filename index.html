About

Meet Timothy Hood

Founder, Fly With Timothy · Private Aviation Advisor

Timothy Hood is the man behind Fly With Timothy, a private aviation service built on personalized travel, real relationships, and seamless execution. Originally from Atlantic City, New Jersey, now based in Las Vegas, his path into aviation started with a friendship, not a plan. A chance connection with Austin Fuentes, Director at Cirrus Aviation, opened the door. Timothy walked through it.

Two years coordinating client travel, sourcing aircraft, and managing the details that make or break a private flight turned an opportunity into a calling. In 2026, he took that experience independent and founded Fly With Timothy, a brand built around one idea: the flight is the easy part. Handling everything else is the job.
